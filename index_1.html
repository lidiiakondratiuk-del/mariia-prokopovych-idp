NOT STARTED ▾
Starting point
The most valuable case on this plan for the work you actually do. On short projects with little testing time you already prioritise by risk instinctively — critical flows first, device coverage cut back to the popular sizes when the deadline arrives — and your estimation vocabulary is good for the level: three-point, planning poker, T-shirt sizing, analogous and expert estimation, timeboxing. What's missing is the formal layer. Impact Analysis didn't come up at all, and on exit criteria the answer stopped at "all cases passed, deadline reached, too many bugs" and needed a hint to connect with acceptance criteria, Definition of Done and the test plan. Steps to learn
